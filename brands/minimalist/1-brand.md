### 7. Claims — OK vs NOT OK (India: ASCI Code + Drugs & Cosmetics Act) `[REGULATED — legal review]`
**OK (documented on site):** ingredient names and %; "formulated for oily/combination skin"; "helps unclog pores / reduce the look of blackheads" (cosmetic wording); SPF 50 / PA++++ with the lab test; dermatologist-supervised patch testing; exact rating and review count; current offer terms, including their conditions (minimum order).
**NOT OK:**
- "Cures / treats / heals acne", "removes acne scars", "stops hair fall", "cures dandruff" (drug claims).
- Consumer-study % figures until you have the report (sample size, method, duration) and it's shown in the ad as ASCI requires.
- "Dermatologist recommended", "clinically proven" without product-specific evidence.
- "No white cast" only if verified, with a caveat about skin tones. "Safe for everyone" is out; the SPF isn't for pregnancy/lactation.
- **Returns:** never imply a money-back guarantee. The policy covers wrong, damaged, expired or empty products within 7 days only.
- **Before/after:** real, unretouched, consented; show the timeframe; "results vary"; no filters.
- **Meta personal-attributes rule:** no "Do you have acne?" or "Your oily skin…". Describe the product and concern instead.
- **Influencers:** ASCI requires "#ad / #collab / #sponsored" labels on paid or gifted creator content.
- **Competitors:** no naming or disparaging without a fair, documented comparison.

### 8. Brand voice
Simple · honest · science-led · no hype. (From the site: "simple, honest, and backed by science".)
Do: lead with the ingredient and %, keep it clean and minimal, state facts. Don't: miracle language, "flawless", "glass skin overnight", fear-based skin shaming.
