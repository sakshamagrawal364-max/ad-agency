### 7. Claims — what's OK vs NOT OK
`[REGULATED — legal review]` **Skincare is a regulated, high-risk ad category.**
- **Cosmetic vs drug line:** claims like "treats acne", "stops hair fall", "removes pigmentation", "cures dandruff" can turn a cosmetic into a drug claim. In the US that's FDA territory (and FTC substantiation); in India, Drugs & Cosmetics rules plus ASCI advertising guidelines. Which rules apply depends on your market. MISSING — ask human.
- **"Efficacious / clinically proven / dermatologist-tested"** need real test data per product.
- **Before/after photos:** real, unretouched, consented; show the timeframe; note that results vary. No filters or editing.
- **Sunscreen:** SPF/PA claims must match the product's actual test results and label.
- **Meta personal-attributes rule:** no "Do you have acne?" or "Your dark spots…" wording. Talk about the product or concern, not the viewer's body.
- **Ageing:** no "reverses ageing" or "removes wrinkles".
- **Safe to say now (if true and on the label):** ingredient names and percentages, what each product is formulated for ("formulated for oily, acne-prone skin"), the transparency mission.
