## Concept details

### MC01 · UGC · "Nobody tells you this"
- **Creator brief:** 20–30, oily/combination skin, has used a BHA before and can honestly describe their own experience (no scripted fake experiences).
- **First frame:** creator close-up, text "Nobody tells you this about salicylic acid." No filters or beauty mode.
- **Primary text:** "Starting a new exfoliant? Start slow, patch test, and know what's in your bottle. Minimalist 2% Salicylic Acid, every ingredient listed. #ad"
- **Headline:** "Start slow. Know what's in it." · **Description:** "₹522 · 30ml" *(live price)*
- **Compliance:** the purge guidance is brand-approved; "If irritation persists, stop use and consult a dermatologist" on screen; Paid-partnership label.

### MC02 · Brand video · Myth-bust
- **Visual:** white background, black serif/sans text (brand's minimal style), product macro between frames.
- **Primary text:** "Purging vs irritation: what to know before you start a BHA."
- **Compliance:** R&D-approved copy only.

### MC03 · Expert explainer
- **Needs:** a real Minimalist R&D or chemistry team member with their real title, on camera with consent. If none is available → drop it.

### MC04 · Product macro
- **Visual:** slow 360° pack spin → label close-up → INCI highlights ("Salicylic Acid 2%"). Clean studio light.
- **Primary text:** "No hidden ingredients. No vague 'complexes'. Just 2% salicylic acid and a full, honest label."

### MC05 · "Read the label with me"
- **Creator brief:** curious, explainer-style. Uses the brand-approved ingredient glossary.
- **Visual:** creator holding the bottle to camera, label text animated on screen.

### MC06 · Static contrast
- **Layout:** split card: left, a generic blurred label reading "Proprietary complex" (no real brand); right, the Minimalist pack, "Salicylic Acid 2%".
- **Headline:** "Know what you're using."

### MC07 · GRWM 3-step
- **Visual:** night (BHA → B5) and morning (SPF) shown in real time; product names and % on screen.
- **Primary text:** "3 bottles. 3 jobs. Every ingredient listed. Buy 2, get the 3rd free." *(if live)*
- **Compliance:** SPF claim "SPF 50 PA++++ (lab-tested, ISO 24444:2019)" with the report on file; the SPF is not for pregnancy/lactation per the brand FAQ.

### MC08 · Carousel "3 steps"
- **Cards:** ① BHA 2%: helps unclog pores · ② B5 10%: hydration · ③ SPF 50 PA++++: sun protection · ④ offer card with terms.

### MC09 · UGC "My nose by 3pm"
- **Visual:** creator's real T-zone shine at the end of a day (honest, unretouched), then the routine.
- **Compliance:** first person only. Rating quoted exactly with the date.

### MC10 · Macro demo
- **Visual:** dropper → drop on glass → texture spread; text "2% BHA · oil-soluble" `[VERIFY WITH BRAND]`.

### MC11 · Offer static
- **Layout:** 3 products in a row, "Buy 2, Get 3rd Free", offer T&Cs in small print. Price only if live.

### MC12 · Cart walkthrough
- **Visual:** real screen recording of beminimalist.co with the cart total; re-film if the price or offer changes.

### MC13 · Testimonial: ⚠️ CONDITIONAL
- Needs real customers who give **written consent** to use their quote/video in ads. Show "Results vary". Customers must not be paid without disclosure. No scripting of results.

### MC14 · Before-After: ⚠️ CONDITIONAL
- Needs a real customer or creator, **documented** weeks 1–6, same lighting, **no filters/retouching**, written consent, the timeframe shown, "Results vary. Individual experiences differ." Cosmetic framing only ("look of blackheads", "skin texture"). No acne-clearing claims. Brand legal review required.

## Suggested test plan (recommendation only)

**Test 1: Angle test (find the message).** Similar UGC format, different angle.
| Ad | Concept | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| A | MC01 | Honest purge talk (M6) |
| B | MC05 | Know what's in it (M3) |
| C | MC07 | 3-step routine (M14) |
| D | MC09 | Blackhead reality (M1) |

**Test 2: Hook test on the Test 1 leader.** 4 new first-3-second hooks, same body.
**Test 3: Offer test.** Single serum vs routine bundle (MC11/MC12) landing on the same page.

**Hold constant:** landing page (product page vs bundle page; choose one per test), offer, placements, audience, creator style.
**Metrics to watch:** primary KPI is **CPA** (cost per purchase). For diagnosis also track hook rate (3s views ÷ impressions), hold rate, CTR, CPC, add-to-cart rate, and average order value (important for routine angles).
**Minimum data you may want before judging:** a rough rule of thumb is ≥1,000 impressions per ad for attention metrics, and ~20–30+ purchases per ad before trusting CPA differences. Below that, treat results as directional.

**Options for YOU to choose (not spending recommendations):**
- Audience: broad (India, 18+, matching the SA serum's 18+ label), interest-based (skincare, Nykaa, beauty), lookalikes from purchaser lists (only if the brand provides data and consent rules allow).
- Age floor: **18+**, since the SA serum page says 18+.
- Structure: one ad set with 4 ads, or the platform's A/B test tool. Budget, schedule and bids are your decision.
