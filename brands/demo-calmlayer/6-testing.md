## Analysis (Testing Agent) — 🚨 PRACTICE ONLY

### 1. Data inventory
- 2 arms, 10 days, $500 each ($1,000 total), identical ad, so differences after the click are attributable to the page (within noise).
- **New this round:** landing-page views (LPV), so page-level conversion can be measured for the first time.
- **DATA NOT PROVIDED:** page load time, bounce rate/scroll depth, device split, refunds, margin, repeat rate.

### 2. Results table (all derived metrics **(calculated)**)

Ad-side metrics (CTR, hook rate) are identical, which confirms the split worked: same ad, same audience quality.

### 3. Confidence check
- **Purchases: 27 vs 36.** The best volume so far, but still modest.
- Two-proportion z-tests (calculated):
  - **Add to cart ÷ LPV: p ≈ 0.012.** LP-B gets more visitors to add to cart. **Moderate confidence.**
  - Purchases ÷ LPV: p ≈ 0.12 · purchases ÷ clicks: p ≈ 0.22 · purchases ÷ impressions: p ≈ 0.23. **Not significant.** The CPA gap (−25%) is directional.
  - **LPV rate: p ≈ 0.0007.** LP-B **loses more clicks before the page loads** (80.1% vs 87.0%). **High confidence.** Likely the autoplay video slowing the load (hypothesis).
- **Cross-round check:** the same ad (R3C02) on page A cost **$18.52** this round vs **$14.29** in Round 3. Its Round 3 lead over R2C01 (~$18) may have been partly noise. This supports the earlier caution that low-confidence CPA leads can shrink when re-run.
- Frequency is ~1.37 (up from 1.32). Same audience, fourth round.

### 4. Summary
- **LP-B leads on the primary KPI:** CPA $13.89 vs $18.52 (calculated, −25%), ROAS 1.94 vs 1.46.
- **Where it wins:** once visitors arrive, LP-B converts better (LP-CVR 6.61% vs 4.50%; ATC 21.7% vs 15.8%, moderate confidence on ATC).
- **Where it loses:** ~7 points more clicks drop off before the page loads (high confidence). **LP-B is winning despite leaking visitors**, so fixing the load may widen the gap (hypothesis).
- **Reality check:** R3C02's Round 3 CPA didn't repeat on page A. Treat every single-round CPA lead as provisional.

### 5. Diagnosis (hypotheses, not facts)
- **Message match:** visitors see the same rub test and guarantee they just saw in the ad, which may reduce hesitation (ATC lift).
- **Load cost:** the above-the-fold video likely added load time, which may explain the lower LPV rate. A poster image that swaps to video after load, or a lighter file, could recover those visitors.
- **Post-click ceiling partly lifted:** LP-B pushed purchases ÷ clicks to 5.29%, above the ~3.6–3.95% ceiling seen in Rounds 2–3 (practice).
