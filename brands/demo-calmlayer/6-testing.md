### 5. Confidence check
- **5A purchases: 36 / 39 / 47.** **5B: 27 / 30.**
- Two-proportion z-tests (calculated):
  - **C vs A, purchases ÷ impressions: p ≈ 0.16.** Not significant. A −23% CPA is directional, though the largest gap in several rounds.
  - **C vs A, hook rate: p < 0.001 · CTR: p ≈ 0.0003.** The patch-test opening and its clicks beat the control with **high confidence**. It's the first new hook to beat the rub test since Round 2 (practice).
  - C vs A, purchases ÷ LPV: p ≈ 0.57. On-page conversion is similar, so **C's CPA edge comes mainly from attention and clicks.**
  - **B vs A, hook rate: p < 0.001 (B lower).** The label reveal is weaker at stopping scrolls (high confidence) · purchases ÷ LPV: p ≈ 0.41 · purchases ÷ impressions: p ≈ 0.85. **B attracts fewer but possibly more purchase-ready visitors**; not significant.
  - **5B, audience 2 vs 1, purchases ÷ impressions: p ≈ 0.33** · CTR p ≈ 0.18 · purchases ÷ LPV p ≈ 0.46. **Not significant.** Audience 2 costs more per 1,000 impressions ($16.67 vs $14.29) but converts better; the net effect is within noise.
- **Frequency:** the broad audience reached **1.48** (the sixth run). The fresh audience started at **1.14**. As expected, but only a signal.
- **Profitability caveat:** the margin calculations depend entirely on the **practice** unit-economics inputs. The repeat-rate benefit assumes repeats need no ad spend.

### 6. Summary
- **5A:** **R5C02 (patch test)** has the lowest CPA ($10.64) and the healthiest practice margin (+$4.47 per first order). Its attention gains are high confidence; its CPA lead is directional (p≈0.16). **A new angle produced a bigger gap than the Round 4 tweaks**, as Step 7 suggested it might.
- **R5C01 (label reveal):** weaker hook but the best on-page conversion. It may suit retargeting or audiences that already know the brand (hypothesis, untested).
- **5B:** the skincare-interest audience is **slightly** better on CPA with a much lower frequency. Not significant, but it gives an alternative to the saturating broad audience.
- **Profitability:** thin but positive on first orders at current CPAs (practice inputs). **Not a comfortable margin for scaling**, which would typically push CPA up (hypothesis).

### 7. Diagnosis (hypotheses, not facts)
- **Why the patch test may work:** it names and shows the exact fear sensitive-skin buyers have (reacting), and then answers it with a habit + guarantee. A strong match to research pain #4 (trial anxiety).
- **Why the label reveal converts after the click but not before:** text-focused footage is less scroll-stopping, but people who stop to read it may be more deliberate buyers.
- **Audience:** a fresh, interest-based audience may simply be less fatigued, rather than inherently better. More time would show whether its advantage holds as its frequency rises.
