### 3. Confidence check
- **Purchases: 38 / 33 / 42.** The highest volume yet; all are above the ~30 mark for the first time.
- Two-proportion z-tests (calculated):
  - **C vs A, purchases ÷ impressions: p ≈ 0.67** · purchases ÷ LPV: p ≈ 0.50 · ATC ÷ LPV: p ≈ 0.36. **Not significant.** The new CTA's −10% CPA (calculated) is within noise.
  - **B vs A, CTR: p ≈ 0.003 · hold rate: p < 0.001.** The creator again wins attention (**high confidence**, replicating Round 3).
  - B vs A, purchases ÷ LPV: p ≈ 0.29 · purchases ÷ impressions: p ≈ 0.68. Not significant. The creator's conversion gap narrowed vs Round 3 (practice) but persists.
- **Speed-fix check:** the LPV rate is back to ~86% for all ads. Control on LP-B2 at 86.3% vs LP-B in Phase 4A at 80.1%: **p ≈ 0.003 (high confidence the drop-off was recovered)**, and it's in line with page A's 87.0% (p ≈ 0.71).
- **Control stability:** R3C02 CPA $13.16 on LP-B2 vs $13.89 on LP-B (4A). Consistent, which supports LP-B as a real improvement.
- **Frequency:** 1.41, up again (1.22 → 1.32 → 1.37 → 1.41). The fifth round on one audience.

### 4. Summary
- **The page work held:** with the speed fix, the control converts as well as on LP-B while losing far fewer clicks. Control CPA ~$13 vs ~$18 on the old page A (practice).
- **CTA wording (C):** the lowest CPA ($11.90) and best on-page conversion, but **not distinguishable from the control** at this volume. A *possible* edge, not a finding.
- **Creator (B):** for the second round running, the best attention (CTR, hold), the cheapest clicks, and weaker on-page conversion. Even on a page built for the ad's message, the founder version converts better per visitor (directional).
- **Control (A):** the most stable performer across Rounds 3–4.

### 5. Diagnosis (hypotheses, not facts)
- **C:** "Use the whole jar. Then decide." may set clearer expectations (permission to try fully), reflected in higher ATC→purchase (31.1%). Could also be noise.
- **B:** the creator draws curious, engaged viewers. The page still speaks in the founder's voice, so the creator-matched page (R4-02 V3) remains the untested explanation.
- **Diminishing returns on ad variants:** three ads with near-identical hook rates (~37%) and similar CPAs suggest this creative family is close to its ceiling with this audience. Rising frequency adds to that picture.
