### 3. Confidence check
- **Purchases: 22 / 19 / 28 / 18.** For the first time, two ads (A, C) passed ~20, but **all are still at the low end** of the ~20–30 rule of thumb. CPA comparisons remain **LOW-TO-MODERATE confidence at best.**
- Two-proportion z-tests (calculated):
  - **C vs A, purchases ÷ impressions: p ≈ 0.43. Not significant.** The early guarantee's CPA lead (−21%) could still be noise.
  - C vs A, purchases ÷ clicks: p ≈ 0.35 · ATC ÷ clicks: p ≈ 0.26. Not significant, though every C funnel step points the same way.
  - B vs A, hook rate: **p < 0.001**. The stacked hook stops more scrolls (40.3% vs 37.3%) · purchases: p ≈ 0.68, no difference.
  - D vs A, CTR: **p ≈ 0.008** · hold rate: **p < 0.001**. The creator holds attention and earns clicks better · purchases ÷ clicks: p ≈ 0.36, no clear difference.
  - C vs D, purchases ÷ impressions: p ≈ 0.24.
- **Frequency** rose to ~1.32 (from ~1.22). Still low, but the same audience is in its third round. Worth watching.
- **Control stability (practice):** R2C01 CPA went from $17.65 (R2) to $18.18 (R3). Consistent, which supports it as a fair baseline.

### 4. Summary
- **Primary KPI (CPA):** **C / R3C02 (guarantee at 3s)** leads at $14.29, about 21% below the control (calculated). It's best at every step after the click: CVR, ATC rate, ATC→purchase. **Not statistically confirmed.**
- **B / R3C01 (stacked hook):** the best hook rate, but a *lower* hold rate and CVR. The extra 1.5s of empty jar may delay the demo. CPA is worse than the control.
- **D / R3C03 (creator):** the best CTR, CPC and hold rate (high confidence on those), but the *lowest* CVR. Clicks may be more curiosity-driven, or the page may not continue the creator's voice.
- **Control A:** steady.

### 5. Diagnosis (hypotheses, not facts)
- **C supports the Round 2 lesson.** Showing the guarantee during the demo, rather than near the end, may convert hesitant viewers before they leave. Three practice rounds now point the same way: guarantee early > guarantee late > guarantee only at the end.
- **B: hook rate isn't the bottleneck any more.** The control already stops ~37% of viewers. Squeezing more out of the first second added viewers who didn't stay (hold 27.7%).
- **D: attention ≠ purchase intent.** The creator earns the most engaged viewing and clicks, but the landing page is founder- and brand-voiced. Possibly a message-match gap, or a creator audience that's less ready to buy.
- **Landing page is still untested (R3-07).** CVR differences between ads with similar CTRs (C 5.09% vs D 2.95%) suggest that what happens after the click matters a lot.
