### R5C01 · Live label reveal · 20s
| # | Time | Shot | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3s | Macro, real time: a hand slowly rotates the jar; the camera tracks the INCI list around the label (focus on text, not logo) | "The entire ingredient list. 14 ingredients." | Soft jar-turn sound |
| 2 | 3–8s | The rotation continues (one continuous take ≥ 8s) | **Lower third:** "60-day money-back guarantee, even if the jar is empty. See site for terms." | — |
| 3 | 8–11s | Founder, mid-shot (R3C02 set) | — | "Sensitive skin made me suspicious of long ingredient lists." `[VERIFY: true for founder]` |
| 4 | 11–14s | Founder holds the jar, label to camera | Ingredient names animate in as spoken | "So CalmLayer has 14: ceramides, cholesterol, niacinamide, panthenol, squalane… and no fragrance." |
| 5 | 14–17s | Label close-up, real INCI; highlight boxes appear on each named ingredient | "No fragrance · No essential oils" | — |
| 6 | 17–20s | Founder → jar on neutral background | CTA headline `[HUMAN PICK]` + "See site for terms" | "Read it all. Then try it for 60 days." |

**Specs:**
- The label must be **legible on a phone**: shoot at 4K and crop, with even lighting and no glare on the jar. Test by watching on a phone at arm's length.
- The highlight boxes must sit on the **real** printed text (no mock label).
- If the rotation take is < 8s, shoot a slower, longer take; don't loop footage to fake length.

**Platform copy:**
- Primary text: "14 ingredients. All of them on the label, and all of them here: ceramides, cholesterol, niacinamide, panthenol, squalane… No fragrance. And a 60-day money-back guarantee, even if the jar is empty."
- Headline: `[HUMAN PICK]` · Description: "15% off your first order with email signup" (if live)

**Compliance:** INCI = the real pack, character for character · no claim that fewer ingredients prevents reactions · the founder's line must be true.

### R5C02 · Patch test on camera · 20s
| # | Time | Shot | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3s | Close-up, real time: the founder dabs a pea-sized amount on the side of their jaw (natural light, no filter or beauty mode) | "How I try anything new on sensitive skin." *(the fallback hook; see note)* | — |
| 2 | 3–8s | The dab spreads into a small patch (same take, ≥ 8s) | **Lower third:** guarantee + terms | — |
| 3 | 8–11s | Founder to camera | — | "Trying a new moisturizer always felt like a gamble for me." |
| 4 | 11–14s | Founder points to the small patch area | "Patch test first" | "So I patch test first, a small spot for a few days, before using it all over." `[VERIFY]` |
| 5 | 14–17s | Founder holds the jar | "Fragrance-free · 14 ingredients" | "CalmLayer is fragrance-free, with 14 ingredients, and you've got 60 days to decide." |
| 6 | 17–20s | Jar on neutral background | CTA headline `[HUMAN PICK]` + **"Discontinue use if irritation occurs."** + "See site for terms" | — |

**Hook choice:** this brief uses the **fallback** hook ("How I try anything new on sensitive skin.") rather than "Sensitive skin? Here's how I try anything new." The fallback is first-person and avoids any reading as a statement about the viewer. Switch back only if legal clears the original.

**Specs:**
- Natural, even daylight; skin shown as it is (no retouching, no smoothing).
- The patch-test wording must match verified guidance before the founder records the VO.

**Platform copy:**
- Primary text: "I patch test anything new before using it all over. CalmLayer is fragrance-free with 14 ingredients, and you've got 60 days to decide, even if the jar is empty. Discontinue use if irritation occurs."
- Headline: `[HUMAN PICK]` · Description: "15% off your first order with email signup" (if live)

**Compliance:** no "won't irritate"/"safe for all sensitive skin" · the patch test is presented as the founder's habit and general guidance, not a guarantee · the "discontinue use" line is on screen and in the copy.
