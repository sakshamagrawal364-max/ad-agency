**Insight:** the category is dominated by **dermatologist-associated drugstore brands** (CeraVe, Vanicream, LRP) on value and trust. A $28 D2C newcomer can't win on "derm-backed" or on price. It has to win on **feel/texture + simplicity + risk reversal** (guarantee).

### 2. Pain points
1. **Stinging/burning.** Often linked to a compromised barrier or ingredient sensitivity; common triggers are "fragrance, drying alcohols, and harsh preservatives" ([Green People](https://www.greenpeople.co.uk/blogs/the-beauty-hub/why-do-moisturisers-make-my-skin-sting), commercial source).
2. **Heaviness/greasiness.** A cream "felt suffocating" ([Sephora Community](https://community.sephora.com/t5/Skincare-Aware/Bad-reaction-to-SkinFix/m-p/6151100)).
3. **Pilling.** A moisturizer "felt heavy and pilled when rubbed in" ([Sephora Community](https://community.sephora.com/t5/Skincare-Aware/Looking-for-a-moisturizer-that-doesn-t-pill/m-p/6757973)).
4. **Trial anxiety:** "the most sensitive skin, need recs" style threads; fear of reacting to a new product ([Sephora Community](https://community.sephora.com/t5/Skincare-Aware/The-most-sensitive-skin-need-recs/m-p/5944017/highlight/true)).
5. **Too rich vs too light:** LRP "may not be rich enough"; others too heavy, so a texture gap.

### 3. Desires
Comfort with no sting · lightweight but enough · works under SPF/makeup · fragrance-free · simple, short ingredient lists · not having to think about it.

### 8. Messaging gap
- **Saturated:** "dermatologist recommended", "repairs your barrier", ceramide science lectures (big brands own these).
- **Opportunity:**
  1. **Texture & comfort as proof:** show the no-sting, no-pill experience on camera (sensory demo) rather than claiming barrier science.
  2. **Risk reversal:** "60 days, even if the jar's empty." Directly answers sensitive-skin trial anxiety. Big drugstore brands don't lead with this (ASSUMPTION — check Ad Library).
  3. **"Fewer ingredients, fewer surprises":** short INCI as reassurance.
- **Compliance risk:** "repair" language. Prefer "supports / helps / comfortable".
