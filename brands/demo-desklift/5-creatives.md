## Concept details

### C01 · UGC · "Try to make it wobble"
- **Visual:** tripod, desk-level; first frame = hands hovering over the keyboard at max height, text "Max height. Typing hard."; one continuous take; screen reflection shows movement.
- **Primary text:** "We tried to make it wobble. One take, no edits. 6 heights, folds flat, locks in place."
- **Headline:** "The stand that stays put" `[only if the demo proves it]` · **Description:** "Free shipping · 30-day returns"
- **Production:** founder, phone, real DeskLift, any 13–16" laptop.
- **Compliance:** caption "Unedited, one take" must be literally true.

### C02 · Video · Side-by-side test
- **Visual:** split-screen, timestamp overlay, same laptop/height; old stand unbranded or logo covered.
- **Primary text:** "Same laptop. Same height. Same typing. Judge for yourself."
- **Headline:** "See the difference" · **Description:** "30-day returns"
- **Compliance:** a fair test; no named competitor.

### C03 · Founder story
- **Visual:** founder at desk, natural light; B-roll of the hinge macro and folding into a bag.
- **Primary text:** "I designed DeskLift around one thing: a stand that doesn't wobble when you actually type."
- **Compliance:** the story must be true `[VERIFY WITH FOUNDER]`.

### C04 · UGC · Bouncing screen callout
- **Visual:** slow-mo bounce (labelled "slow motion") → real-time DeskLift shot.
- **Primary text:** "If your screen bounces when you type, it's the stand, not you."

### C05 · Static · Bold statement
- **Layout:** big headline top; product photo at max height with a laptop; 3 spec chips (6 heights · folds flat · 11–16"); "Free shipping" badge.
- **Headline:** "Your laptop stand shouldn't dance." · **CTA:** Get Offer.
- **Compliance:** spec chips only, no superlatives.

### C06 · POV video call
- **Visual:** dramatized jittery call → steady call. On-screen "Dramatization".
- **Primary text:** "Stop the shake on your next call."

### C07 · UGC · Desk-solid, bag-small
- **Visual:** fold → backpack sleeve → café unfold → typing test.
- **Primary text:** "One stand for your desk and your bag."
- **Compliance:** no dimensions until verified.

### C08 · Day-in-the-life
- **Visual:** 3 locations, clock overlay; real setups only.

### C09 · Static · Books vs DeskLift
- **Layout:** left = book stack (❌ adjustable ❌ portable ❌ stable); right = DeskLift (✅ 6 heights ✅ folds flat ✅ locking hinge).

### C10 · UGC meme
- **Visual:** book stack slides → DeskLift appears. Trending audio (check licensing for commercial use).

### C11 · Before-After (setup only)
- **Layout:** identical desk photos: laptop flat vs on DeskLift; a line showing screen height. No people's bodies, no posture/health outcome text.
- **Primary text:** "Same desk. Same laptop. Screen at a better height."
- **Compliance:** real, unaltered photos; a setup change only, not a body or health result.

### C12 · Buyer's guide
- **Visual:** 3 numbered checks, each shown with a real demo.

### C13 · Testimonial: ❌ NOT PRODUCIBLE YET
- Template only. Needs real customers who have given written consent. If a result is shown, add a typical-results note. No paid actors posing as customers unless disclosed.

## Suggested test plan (recommendation only)

**Test 1: Hook test (isolate the first 3 seconds).** Same angle family (stability), similar body.
| Ad | Concept | Variable |
|---|---|---|
| A | C01 | Pattern-interrupt hook |
| B | C04 | Callout hook |
| C | C06 | POV hook |
| D | C02 | Challenge hook |

**Test 2: Angle test (later).** C01 (Proof) vs C07 (Desire) vs C10 (Comparison) vs C11 (Before-After).

**Hold constant:** offer (free shipping), landing page, CTA button (where possible), audience (your choice), placement setup.
**Metrics to watch:** your primary KPI is CPA. For diagnosis also track hook rate (3s views ÷ impressions), CTR, cost per click, and add-to-cart.
**Minimum data you may want before judging:** a rough rule of thumb is ≥1,000 impressions per ad for attention metrics, and ~20–30+ purchases per ad before trusting CPA differences. Below that, treat results as directional.

**Audience / budget / placement options for YOU to choose (not recommendations to spend):**
- Audience options: broad (let the platform find buyers), interest-based (remote work, productivity, travel), lookalike (only once you have customer data).
- Budget structure options: one campaign/ad set with 4 ads, or ad-level testing tools on the platform. The amount is your decision.
