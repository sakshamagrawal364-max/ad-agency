### 7. Claims
**OK to say (if specs verified):** adjustable to 6 heights; folds flat; aluminum; fits 11–16" laptops; raises your screen; free shipping; 30-day returns.
**Must NOT say:**
- "Cures / fixes / eliminates neck or back pain", "doctor/chiropractor-approved", "prevents injury". These are health claims with no substantiation, and DeskLift is not a medical device.
- "Zero wobble" / "the most stable stand", until it has actually been tested.

- Comparative claims vs named competitors without a fair, documented test.
- Softer wording like "Designed for a more comfortable, eye-level posture" is acceptable but health-adjacent. Keep it general.
