## Top 5 shortlist
1. **A7 Watch It Not Wobble (Proof):** turns the most common category complaint into a visible demo.
2. **A1 The Shake (Problem):** names the pain in buyers' own words ("wobbly when typing").
3. **A3 Desk-Solid, Bag-Small (Desire):** the positioning gap between the Roost and the mStand.
4. **A17 Books vs Stand (Comparison):** low-risk, relatable, cheap to film.
5. **A16 Fixed vs Folding (Comparison):** helps people who are comparing; compares categories, not named brands.

Deliberately not prioritized: **A2** (posture/neck messaging is saturated, and health claims are risky) and **A8** (no real reviews yet).
