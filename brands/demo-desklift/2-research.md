**Where DeskLift sits:** at $59 it's between the Nexstand (~$30) and the Roost (~$80–90). It is
adjustable and portable like those two, but aluminum like the mStand. The intended gap is

### 2. Pain points (ranked)
1. **Wobble / screen shake while typing.** This is the most repeated complaint across stands.
   - Budget stands described as "slightly wobbly when typing directly on laptop keyboard" ([droid4x](https://droid4x.com/best-laptop-stands-for-neck-pain-relief/))
   - "Slightly wobbly at max height" ([dev.to](https://dev.to/vinay_suneja/laptop-stands-do-you-actually-need-one-20-80-comparison-5p0))
   - A leg "a couple millimeters shorter than the other" makes it rock on a flat desk ([Thomann reviews](https://www.thomann.co.uk/hercules_stands_hcdg_400bb_laptop_stand_reviews.htm?rating=2))
   - A larger laptop "might be a little wobbly when typing with a higher angle" ([Best Buy](https://bestbuy.com/site/reviews/insignia-laptop-stand-with-adjustable-height-and-angle-for-laptops-up-to-17-wide-silver/6604298))
2. **Looking down at a low screen / neck strain.** Widely discussed, but mostly by sellers (commercial bias) ([Contour Design](https://www.contourdesign.com/workstations/laptop-posture-stand), [CMD](https://www.cmd-ltd.com/advice-centre/ergonomics/laptop-at-home-ergonomics/)). ⚠️ Health-adjacent, so use only general language in ads.
3. **Durability: hinges and parts fail.**
   - Hinge so tight a ball bearing was "forced out of its track" ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/stxnd.com))
   - "Fine for the price, broke easily" ([Thomann](https://www.thomannmusic.com/millenium_laptopstand_reviews.htm?rating=3))
   - Rubber nubs "keep popping off"; "a lot of play in the joints" ([Thomann](https://www.thomann.co.uk/reloop_stand_hub_reviews.htm?rating=2))
4. **Portability hassle.** "You have to disassemble it for transport… I'm afraid of losing the screws" ([Thomann](https://www.thomannmusic.com/millenium_laptopstand_reviews.htm?rating=3))
5. **Fit.** "Wasn't wide enough for our computer" ([Staples](https://www.staples.com/ptd/review/IM1JL7687))
